# Changelog

All notable changes to this module. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/); versions follow [SemVer](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0]

First release.

- Standing with every faction: attitude toward the party (Hostile / Indifferent / Friendly), renown for the party and for each hero (the 2024 DMG Renown rule: never below 0, recognition at 3+, ranks and perks at thresholds), membership and ranks, next rank, downtime to the next point, level-based renown, DMG award buttons.
- Contacts, with faction, role, attitude, what they offer and when they were met; drop an actor to make one.
- A ledger of debts and favours: with a faction, a contact or a name; the party or named heroes; weight; due; open / called in / paid / broken / forgiven, with a log per entry.
- Everything hidden from the players until revealed; GM-only debts.
- The players' Standing window (read-only), and a button to open it on every player's screen.
- Journal buttons: `@Faction`, `@Renown`, `@Attitude`, `@Debt`, `@Favour`, `@Ledger`.
- History of every change with session and reason; a GM journal page rebuilt after each change (a Planar Codex note when the Codex is active).
- Chat announcements (GM only, everyone for known things, or off).
- JSON packs: factions, ranks, perks, missions, contacts and ledger seeds. A generic pack ships with the module (no factions).
- Macro API. English and Greek.
